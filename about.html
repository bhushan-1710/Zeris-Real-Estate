The Core Philosophy

"Honesty, Integrity, and Efficiency"

Buying or renting property in Mumbai is rarely just a transaction. Whether you are acquiring a high-floor sea-facing duplex in Versova or a quiet mountain bungalow in Lonavala, it represents either your day-to-day base or your private family retreat.

Yet finding real estate across Mumbai's Western Suburbs and Lonavala often comes with inflated claims, unverified carpet areas, and unnecessary middleman friction. Zeris Real Estate was created to give buyers and tenants a straightforward alternative: accurate numbers, clean paperwork, and fast, direct communication without the noise.

Honesty

Precise carpet area figures, actual maintenance costs, society NOC rules, and clear pricing benchmarks. No inflated estimates or bait-and-switch listings.

Integrity

Thorough title verification, encumbrance checks, and complete clarity on transfer fees and registration costs before any token amount is discussed.

Efficiency

Direct WhatsApp coordination, quick viewing schedules, organized digital paperwork, and smooth registration support that respects your schedule.

Founder

Sajid Zeri

At just 25, Sajid Zeri stepped into the dynamic world of real estate—not by chance, but by choice, vision, and an unshakable belief in creating something meaningful. Growing up in Versova, Mumbai, surrounded by the hum of change and development, Sajid was always intrigued by the stories buildings held—of dreams, families, ambitions, and legacies.

While many around him pursued conventional paths, Sajid was drawn to the raw, real pulse of the property market. With no large capital but plenty of courage, he began his journey by helping friends and family with their rental and sale needs, earning trust through honesty and hustle. What set him apart wasn’t just his knowledge of the area—it was his people-first approach. He listened more than he spoke, understood needs before pitching options, and treated every deal like it was his own home being bought or sold.

Zeris Real Estate was born out of this ethos—crafted to be more than a brokerage, it became a trusted name built on relationships. Today, Sajid continues to grow his brand with humility and hunger, redefining real estate in Versova with a youthful perspective and old-school integrity. His story is proof that age is no barrier when your roots are strong and your intentions are clear.

Two Skylines · One Relationship

One Broker for Two Very Different Paces

Most brokerages focus exclusively on city apartments or separate weekend homes. Zeris bridges both because our clients live across both rhythms.

During the week, you navigate the fast pace of Versova, Lokhandwala, and Bandra—where location, transit, and social infrastructure define value. On weekends, you seek the cool mist and quiet of Tungarli and Lonavala—where open plots, private lawns, and mountain air matter most.

Working with one trusted advisor who understands both the pace you live at in the city and what you need in a hillside home keeps your property decisions simple, transparent, and grounded.
