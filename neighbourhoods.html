Operating Geography

Four Micro-Markets

From the artistic coastal lanes of Versova and the high-energy towers of Lokhandwala to the storied luxury of Linking Road Bandra and the mist-shrouded hillsides of Lonavala.

Mumbai Western Coast

Versova, Andheri West

Versova is Mumbai's most distinct residential coastline. Home to film, literary, and media creators, its architecture blends low-rise co-operative enclaves with modern sea-facing towers. Known for tranquil Yari Road lanes, artisanal cafes, fresh coastal sea air, and intimate community living.

3 Min Versova Metro Line 1
8 Min Juhu Beach Promenade
12 Min Coastal Road Access
Typical Property Archetypes Duplex Residences · Sea-Facing 2/3BHKs · Penthouse Terraces

Cosmopolitan Suburb Core

Lokhandwala Complex

One of Mumbai's most self-contained and vibrant residential ecosystems. Lokhandwala offers premier gated complexes, expansive multi-tier clubhouses, premium retail promenades, and serene back-road residential pockets with excellent connectivity to western commercial arteries.

4 Min Infinity Mall & High Street
6 Min Kokilaben Hospital
10 Min Western Express Highway
Typical Property Archetypes Gated Tower Apartments · 3 & 4BHK High Rises · Commercial Showrooms

Premier Luxury Corridor

Linking Road, Bandra

The benchmark of prestige and capital stability in Mumbai's western suburbs. Bandra balances iconic heritage villas with cutting-edge architectural luxury towers. Seamless access to BKC, South Mumbai, international schools, and premier lifestyle institutions.

6 Min Bandra-Worli Sea Link
14 Min BKC Financial Hub
22 Min International Airport
Typical Property Archetypes Ultra-Luxury Floors · Redeveloped Boutiques · Prime High-Street Assets

Weekend Hillside Escape

Tungarli & Lonavala Hills

The antidote to Mumbai's relentless pace. Situated just 90 minutes via the Expressway, Lonavala's Tungarli, Gold Valley, and hillside perches offer freehold bungalow plots, misty valleys, crisp monsoon climates, and private family sanctuaries designed for unhurried living.

8 Min Expressway Toll Exit
18 Min Tiger Point & Viewpoints
90 Min Mumbai via Expressway
Typical Property Archetypes Standalone Hillside Bungalows · Gated Villa Communities · Farmhouse Plots
